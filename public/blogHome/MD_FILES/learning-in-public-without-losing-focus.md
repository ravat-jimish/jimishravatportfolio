# Learning in public without losing focus

Sharing notes and experiments can turn private learning into a useful body of work. It works best when the sharing serves the learning rather than replacing it.

## Keep a small loop

Choose one question, explore it, and publish what changed in your understanding. A short, honest note is often more valuable than a polished summary with no point of view.

Learning in public is not a requirement to have all the answers. It is a way to make progress visible and invite better questions.
