# Designing for the moment after the happy path

Empty states, errors, and slow networks are where product quality becomes visible. They are not edge details; they are normal parts of a real product.

## Make the next step clear

An error should explain what happened and what the person can do now. An empty state should make the first useful action obvious without filling the screen with instructions.

Designing these states early gives engineering better requirements and gives users a calmer experience when things are imperfect.
